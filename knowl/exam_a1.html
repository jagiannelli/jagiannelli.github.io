Example5.1.1

Let \(T\) be a linear transformation from \(\mathbb{R}2 \mapsto \mathbb{R}2\) which is a projection and suppose that

\begin{equation*} T \left(\begin{array}{r} 3 \\ 1 \end{array}\right) = \left(\begin{array}{r} 1 \\ 2 \end{array}\right) \end{equation*}

Find the standard matrix of \(T\) (The Projection Matrix).
