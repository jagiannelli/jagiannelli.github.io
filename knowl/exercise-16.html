The Mean Value Theorem states that if \(f(x)\) is defined and continuous on the interval \([a,b]\) and differentiable on \((a,b)\text{,}\) then there is at least one number \(c\) in the interval \((a,b)\) (that is \(a < c < b\)) such that \begin{equation*} f'(c)=\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b-a} \end{equation*}
