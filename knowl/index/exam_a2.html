Example 5.3.1.

Let \(A\) be a subspace of \(\mathbb{R}3\) of dimension 2 and let \(\vec{b}\) be a fixed non-zero vector in \(\mathbb{R}3\text{.}\)
🔗
Let \(A′\) denote the subset of \(\mathbb{R}3\) consisting of all vectors of the form \(\vec{a} + \vec{b}\) where \(\vec{a}\) is in the subspace \(A\text{.}\)
🔗
For which vectors \(\vec{b}\) is \(A′\) also a subspace? Justify your answer.
