Response to Oscillating Input

Consider the following differential equation:

\begin{equation} \frac{dy}{dt} = ay + \cos\left(\omega t\right)\tag{4.4.9} \end{equation} \begin{equation} y=y(0)\:at\:t=0\tag{4.4.10} \end{equation}

This is the standard growth equation with a sinuosoidal input term \(\cos\left(\omega t\right)\text{.}\)

We look for a particular solutions of the form:

\begin{equation*} y_p = M\cos\left(\omega t\right) + N\sin\left(\omega t\right) \end{equation*}

where \(M\) and \(N\) are constants.

Now try and solve this using SageMath.
